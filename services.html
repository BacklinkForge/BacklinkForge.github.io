Our Backlink Building Services

Manual, transparent off-page SEO work — from foundational profile links to ongoing prospecting and outreach support.

Every service below is performed by hand and documented in a clear report. We do not guarantee rankings or specific authority metrics — what we promise is careful, relevant work you can verify link by link.

Profile Backlink Building

We create complete, credible profiles for you on forums, communities, and platforms relevant to your niche. Each profile is filled out properly — bio, details, and a natural link back to your website — so it looks like a real participant rather than a throwaway account.

  • Manual account creation with complete profile information
  • Platforms selected for relevance to your industry
  • Natural anchor text and link placement
  • Full report with profile URLs

Business Profile Creation

Consistent business listings build trust with both customers and search engines. We create and claim your business profiles on directories and listing sites with accurate, matching details — name, address, phone, hours, and website.

  • Consistent NAP (name, address, phone) across listings
  • Correct categories and business descriptions
  • Logo and photos where the platform supports them
  • List of live listings for your records

Directory Submissions

We hand-submit your website to relevant web directories — general, niche, and local — choosing the right category for each listing and writing unique descriptions rather than copying the same text everywhere.

  • Hand-picked directories relevant to your niche or location
  • Correct categorisation for every submission
  • Varied, natural business descriptions
  • Report of accepted listings

Guest Post Opportunities

Editorial links from real blogs remain among the most valuable you can earn. We research blogs in your niche that accept guest contributions, handle the outreach, and coordinate topics — so your content appears where your audience actually reads.

  • Prospect research focused on your niche
  • Personalised outreach to blog owners and editors
  • Topic suggestions aligned with each blog's audience
  • Transparent reporting on placements secured

Contextual Backlink Acquisition

A link inside a relevant article carries more weight than a link in a footer or sidebar. We find genuine opportunities — resource pages, mention gaps, and content collaborations — where a link to your site adds real value for readers.

  • Opportunities vetted for relevance and quality
  • Links placed in editorial context, not footers or sidebars
  • Outreach handled professionally under your brand
  • Full documentation of each placement

Link Prospecting and Research

Good outreach starts with a good list. We build curated prospect lists for your campaigns — analysing your niche, studying competitor backlink profiles, and qualifying each prospect so your team contacts sites worth pursuing.

  • Niche and competitor backlink analysis
  • Qualified prospects with contact details where available
  • Organised spreadsheet, ready for your outreach
  • Notes on why each prospect fits

Off-Page SEO Support

Ongoing support for teams managing SEO in-house: anchor text planning, link profile reviews, monitoring new and lost links, and practical guidance on keeping your off-page efforts clean and consistent over time.

  • Anchor text strategy and diversification planning
  • Periodic link profile reviews
  • Monitoring for lost or problematic links
  • Plain-language recommendations, no jargon
