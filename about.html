About BacklinkForge

Manual, transparent backlink building for websites that want sustainable off-page SEO.

What We Do

BacklinkForge is a backlink-building service focused on the unglamorous work that actually moves the needle: researching relevant platforms, creating complete and credible profiles, submitting accurate directory listings, finding guest post opportunities, and keeping careful records of everything built.

We work with small businesses, bloggers, startups, and agencies that need dependable off-page support without hiring a full in-house team. Whether you need a foundation of profile and directory links or ongoing prospecting for outreach campaigns, the process is the same — research first, build by hand, report clearly.

How We Work

  • Manual only. Every profile, listing, and submission is created by a person, not a bot. Automated link spam might be fast, but it puts your site at risk.
  • Relevance first. We look for platforms and communities connected to your niche, because a relevant link is worth far more than a random one.
  • Complete profiles. Half-finished profiles get removed. We fill in bios, photos or logos where appropriate, and accurate details so links survive.
  • Honest reporting. You receive a list of every link built — the platform, the URL, and the date. Nothing hidden, nothing inflated.

What We Will Not Do

We believe trust is the whole business, so we are direct about our limits:

  • We do not guarantee rankings, traffic increases, or specific domain authority scores. No honest provider can.
  • We do not use automated tools, link farms, or private blog networks.
  • We do not build links on hacked, adult, gambling, or otherwise inappropriate sites.
  • We do not sell or rent links on other people's websites without disclosure.

Why a Resources Directory?

Alongside client work, we maintain a public backlink resources directory — hundreds of platforms organised by category, with search and filters. It is the same kind of starting list we use in our own research, and it is free to browse. If you prefer to build links yourself, it is a practical place to begin.
