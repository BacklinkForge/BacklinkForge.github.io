Privacy Policy

Last updated: October 2026

Overview

BacklinkForge ("we", "our") operates this website. This is a static website: it has no accounts, no backend database, and we do not run analytics or advertising trackers on it.

Information We Collect

Contact enquiries

If you contact us through the form on our contact page, your message is composed in your own email app and sent directly to our email address. We receive whatever you choose to include — typically your name, email address, and message — and we use it only to respond to your enquiry. We do not sell or share your details with third parties for marketing.

Automatically collected data

Our hosting provider (GitHub Pages) may log basic technical information such as IP addresses for security and operational purposes. We do not control or access those logs for profiling.

Cookies

This website does not set cookies.

External Links

Our resources directory links to third-party websites. We are not responsible for the privacy practices of those sites; please review their policies before creating accounts or sharing information with them.

Data Retention

Enquiry emails are kept for as long as needed to handle your request and any follow-up, then deleted on request.

Your Rights

You may ask us at any time what personal information we hold about you, ask us to correct it, or ask us to delete it, by emailing us via the contact page.

Changes to This Policy
