What Are Backlinks — and Why They Matter

A backlink is simply a link from one website to another. Search engines treat backlinks as votes of confidence: when other sites link to a page, it signals that the page is worth attention. That is why backlinks remain one of the foundations of off-page SEO.

Not all backlinks carry equal weight. A link from a website related to your niche usually matters more than dozens of links from unrelated sites. The clickable words of a link — its anchor text — should also read naturally. Links come in two broad kinds: "dofollow" links, which can pass ranking signals, and "nofollow" links, which do not pass those signals directly but can still bring visitors and make a link profile look natural. A healthy profile normally contains a mix of both.

What hurts is the shortcut: automated submissions, link schemes, and spammy mass posting can damage a site's rankings instead of helping them. The links in this directory are meant to be built by hand, on real platforms, with complete and honest profiles.

Types of Backlinks in This Directory

The directory currently lists 488 resources, organised by the kind of link each platform supports:

  • Profile backlinks (119) — create a complete profile on a forum, community, or platform and add your website link to it.
  • Blogs & Web 2.0 (71) — blogging and article platforms where you can publish content with links back to your site.
  • Forums & Communities (37) — discussion platforms where genuine participation can earn profile and signature links, where the rules allow them.
  • Directories (12) — web and business directories that list your site with accurate name and contact details.
  • Social & Bookmarks (17) — social and bookmarking sites for sharing and saving content with links.
  • Video & Media (10) — video and image platforms that allow a website link on your profile or channel.
  • Classified Ads (5) — ad listing sites where a listing can carry a link to your website.
  • Other (217) — platforms that do not fit a single category above.

Counts change as the directory is updated, so the numbers above are a snapshot, not a promise.

How to Use This Directory

Use the search box and category filter above to find platforms that fit your niche. Then work through them steadily rather than all at once: pick relevant platforms first, complete each profile properly with a real name, bio, and photo where the platform allows it, and always follow that platform's own rules about links. Vary your anchor text and link to different pages of your site, not only the homepage — that looks far more natural to both users and search engines.

Frequently Asked Questions

Will these backlinks guarantee higher rankings?

No. Rankings depend on many factors, including content quality, on-page SEO, and competition. Backlinks are one part of off-page SEO, not a guarantee of any position.

Are all the links in this directory "dofollow"?

No. It varies by platform, and many profile and forum links are nofollow. A natural mix of dofollow and nofollow links is normal, and nofollow links can still bring referral visitors.

Is the directory free to browse?

Yes. Searching and filtering the full list costs nothing. Most platforms themselves are free to join, though some offer paid upgrades you do not need for a basic listing.

Do I need an account on each platform?

On most of them, yes — you need an account to create a profile, publish, or post. Use accurate details and a consistent brand name across platforms.
